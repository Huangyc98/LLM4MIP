How much can LLMs help solve MIPs?

LLM-assisted solver research: 32 optimal and 2 infeasible results across 132 MIPLIB instances

LLMs have shown promise in advancing open problems in mathematics. An unresolved MIP instance poses a similar kind of challenge: its variables, constraints, objective, and data define a concrete open problem. This perspective motivates our central question—can LLMs make real-world MIP solving more effective, especially by accelerating established solver-based workflows through structural interpretation, reformulation, targeted experimentation, and verifiable proof construction?

Starting from a frozen benchmark universe of 217 open MIPLIB instances, we studied 132 using LLM-assisted solver workflows: 112 original cases and 20 subsequent cases used in a workflow comparison. These experiments show how LLM reasoning can augment numerical optimization: LLMs broaden the search for structure, representations, and certificates, while solvers and independent checks provide the numerical bounds and verification needed for trustworthy results.

Conclusion LLM + optimization solver is a powerful workflow for real MIP solving.
