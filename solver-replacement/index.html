Can LLMs fully replace solvers?

Current evidence says no—not in this campaign. It supports a hybrid system in which LLMs broaden the search for structure, computational backends search and bound, and independent checkers decide what survives.

No.

Verdict

Not as the experiment actually worked

The LLM did not independently certify feasibility, produce every global bound, or validate its own reductions. MILP solvers, SAT engines, exact dynamic programs, exhaustive searches, rational arithmetic, and deterministic original-model audits supplied the computational and proof backends.

This is an empirical boundary, not an impossibility theorem. The campaign shows that replacement did not occur; it cannot establish that future systems will never replace a particular backend.

What “solve” meant

Three jobs, three forms of evidence

LLM

Interpret and propose

Recover formulation semantics, connect matrix patterns to algorithms, propose reductions and invariants, design falsification tests, and coordinate certificate construction.

Backend

Search and bound

Find feasible points, optimize reduced models, enumerate finite state spaces, refute cutoffs, and produce numerical or exact lower-bound evidence.

Checker

Accept or reject

Replay proof traces, verify rational certificates, audit every original row, test mapping directions, and stop an attractive claim from being promoted too far.

Acceptance rule: no campaign claim is accepted solely because an LLM states it. A useful hypothesis becomes a result only after a backend produces evidence and an appropriate checker validates its scope.

20-instance comparison

The closest comparison points both ways

Choose a metric. “Skill-guided” means use of one structure-research workflow; it does not mean LLM-only.

The structure-guided arm recorded better selected primals on 11 of 20 instances, four strict improvements over its frozen public baseline, and two exact closures. The comparison arm retained stronger dual bounds on 12 of 20 and smaller common gaps on 11. Strong primal discovery does not replace global bounding; strong branch-and-bound does not automatically reveal a portable structural proof.

Six skill-arm primals were tolerance-indexed. The two comparison-study closures—ns1456591 and neos-3682128-sandon—belong to a separate archive and are not added to the canonical campaign inventory of 30 conclusions.

What would stronger evidence require?

A proper replacement experiment

A causal test should freeze identical MPS hashes, machines, solver versions, threads, seeds, starts, external information, tolerances, and wall-clock budgets. It should also reserve separate budgets for structural analysis, primal construction, dual search, proof production, and independent verification—then score unsupported claims and verification failures, not only final objective values.

Practical recommendation

Use the LLM to enlarge the hypothesis space and compress human research overhead. Use the best available computational backend for search and proof. Keep acceptance outside both.
